## Featured Project: UpCycle

One of the primary projects in this portfolio is **UpCycle**, a clothing marketplace designed specifically for college campuses.

The idea emerged from a common student problem: buying and selling clothing locally is often fragmented across social media and group chats. UpCycle aims to create a centralized platform where students can easily discover affordable secondhand clothing while promoting sustainability within their campus community.

The project includes:

* User research
* Competitive analysis
* User personas
* Wireframes
* High-fidelity designs
* Product strategy
* Ongoing development

Although still in progress, UpCycle represents my end-to-end product design process and how I think through solving real-world problems.

## Design Philosophy

I believe successful products are created by balancing three perspectives:

* **Users:** Understand their goals, frustrations, and behaviors through research.
* **Business:** Align product decisions with measurable value and strategic objectives.
* **Technology:** Design solutions that are practical, scalable, and feasible to build.

Rather than designing features for their own sake, I enjoy asking:

* What problem are we solving?
* Who benefits most?
* How can we measure success?
* What is the simplest solution that creates the greatest impact?
